Privacy Policy – Sri's Workbench (Personal Stylist)

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Sri's Workbench is a personal, non-commercial wardrobe application that uses Pinterest inspiration to generate outfit recommendations.

Information Accessed

The application accesses Pinterest boards and pins authorized by the user, along with clothing images and descriptions added to the digital wardrobe.

How Information Is Used

Pinterest data is used solely to analyze fashion inspiration and generate personalized outfit recommendations. Personal information is not sold or used for advertising.

Data & Security

Access tokens are kept private. Pinterest data and wardrobe information are used only for the application's functionality. If third-party AI services are used, relevant images or information may be shared to generate recommendations.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the Pinterest API. See Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

Users can revoke Pinterest access at any time through their Pinterest account settings. Locally stored application data can be deleted by the application owner.
